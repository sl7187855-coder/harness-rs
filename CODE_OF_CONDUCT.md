# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community regardless of experience level, background, identity, disability, nationality, or hardware budget.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent, but address harmful behavior clearly.
- Critique code, designs, and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Share credit and explain decisions.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's credentials, private addresses, logs, or identifying details.
- Make participation accessible. Concise reproducers and small, focused issues are appreciated, especially from people with limited hardware or time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, deliberate intimidation, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close discussions, or restrict participation for conduct that harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive personal information.
